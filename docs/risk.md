# Risk Management Framework

## Objective
Zyberix ke har project mein risk ko identify aur mitigate karna.

## Protocol
1. **Assessment**: Project shuru hone se pehle potential risks ki list banayein.
2. **Mitigation**: Har risk ke liye ek backup plan (Plan B) ready rakhein.
3. **Audit**: Har mahine compliance check karein.
